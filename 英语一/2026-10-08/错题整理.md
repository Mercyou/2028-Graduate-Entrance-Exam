**作答状态**：6个搭配均已造句；2道长难句完成译文和部分主干标注，**从句栏尚未补全**。日期为学习包日期。

## 搭配表达的修改
1. **flag a discrepancy**：`It's embarrassing when ... flagged` 时态不协调。叙述过去事件可写 `It was embarrassing when our customer flagged a discrepancy between our quotation and the drawing.`
2. **work around a constraint**：`work around a shutdown` 含义不够准确，建议 `work around the limited shutdown window`。
3. **hold off on commissioning**：`during limited installation time` 不够自然。例：`Having to hold off on commissioning when the installation window is already tight is frustrating.`
4. **trace the issue back to**：`my bad memory` 可以理解；如表达记忆不准，`my faulty memory` 更贴切。
5. **sign off on**：新设备前通常需要冠词。例：`Our customer signed off on the new plating line after a two-week FAT.`
6. **keep the site team in the loop**：表达正确，继续关注句子简洁与信息目的。

## 长难句01：Although + because
原句：`Although the replacement valves arrived on schedule, the commissioning team postponed the pressure test because the revised piping drawings had not yet been approved by the client.`
- Main clause：`the commissioning team postponed the pressure test`
- Although-clause：`Although the replacement valves arrived on schedule`
- Because-clause：`because the revised piping drawings had not yet been approved by the client`
- 本次：主谓宾与大意正确，两个从句栏未填，需补写。

## 长难句02：定语+宾语+时间从句
原句：`The engineer who reviewed the control logic confirmed that the pump should remain off until the tank level sensor sends a stable signal.`
- Main clause：`The engineer ... confirmed ...`
- Relative clause：`who reviewed the control logic`
- Object clause：`that the pump should remain off until the tank level sensor sends a stable signal`
- Until-clause：`until the tank level sensor sends a stable signal`
- 易错理解：`remain off until ...` = 在……之前保持关闭。
- 本次：主句主语与谓语正确、译意基本正确，但从句栏需要补写。
